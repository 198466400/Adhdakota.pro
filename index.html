Definitions

What the words mean

Every tier is priced in these units. Nothing is listed below that isn't defined here.

Workflow module
One automation that runs end to end without you: a scheduled script, webhook handler, sync job, or CLI command. Delivered as source, documented, with a rollback path.
Agent personality
A configured AI assistant with a fixed system prompt, tool access, and guardrails, pointed at your data. Runs on your API key or locally through Ollama.
Identity engine
The shell tying modules together: a command surface, shared config, logging, and a start/stop routine so the whole system survives a reboot.

Describe the project

How a build runs

Four steps, in this order

The sequence is the product: nothing is built before the spec is signed, nothing is billed before you've seen what you're buying.

Intake

You describe the problem in writing or on a 20-minute call. No charge, no obligation.

Scope document

You get a written spec: every module named, its inputs and outputs, the delivery format, the fixed price. You approve it or you walk.

Build

Work runs against the spec. Commits land in your own repo as they happen — no surprise at the end.

Handover

Setup doc, walkthrough, and the fix window opens. Anything in the spec that doesn't work gets fixed at no cost.

Answers

Before you send money

What do I own at the end?

All source, in a repository you control, licensed so you can modify and run it without me. No per-seat fees, no phone-home.

What if it doesn't work?

Anything in the approved scope that doesn't work gets fixed inside the support window at no charge. If a build can't be made to meet its own spec, the balance is refunded.

Refunds?

Full refund of the deposit any time before build work starts. After that, refunds cover the unbuilt portion of the scope.

What happens to my data?

Only what you send to get the work done. It sits on encrypted storage, isn't sold or shared, and is deleted on request within 30 days. Builds run on your infrastructure and your API keys, not mine.

Does this site track me?

No third-party analytics, no ad pixels, no cross-site tracking. Settings you choose here are stored on this device only.
